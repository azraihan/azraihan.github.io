I'm a senior-year Computer Science and Engineering student at BUET, working on computer vision, representation learning, multimodal learning, and world models.

My research asks what vision and multimodal models actually learn to represent, and how to use that knowledge to build models that are more reliable and that respect the structure of the physical world. Much of my work looks inside these models to find where their capabilities emerge and what shapes them. I also build methods that put those insights to use, from teaching networks which symmetries to break to checking what world models rely on from memory. Right now, I'm studying what self-supervised objectives lead encoders to learn, and what they lead them to miss. In my PhD, I hope to build world models whose internal representations we can inspect, understand, and trust.

I am open to PhD opportunities for Fall 2027.
